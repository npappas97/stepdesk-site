Safety comes first

A walking pad is a moving machine. Keep your pad's own remote or stop button within reach every time you use it, and follow the safety instructions from your pad's maker.

  • StepDesk sends commands to your pad over Bluetooth. Bluetooth can drop, be delayed or fail, and a pad may not respond to a command. StepDesk cannot guarantee that the belt will start, stop or change speed when you ask.
  • Do not rely on StepDesk as your only way to stop the belt.
  • Keep children and pets away from the pad, and do not leave a running pad unattended.
  • You use your walking pad, and StepDesk with it, at your own risk.
