Support

Email deskcomrade64@gmail.com. In the app, Settings has Share support log, which lets you send the log with your message. The log is only shared if you choose to.

Which pads work?

Works with walking pads that use the PitPat app. Pads that use other apps are not supported yet.

The app can't find my pad

Turn the pad on, stand it close to your iPhone, and allow Bluetooth for StepDesk in Settings (StepDesk shows an Open Settings button if Bluetooth permission is off). Close the PitPat app, since a pad usually talks to one app at a time.

My walks are not in Apple Health

Open Settings, Health, Data Access & Devices, StepDesk, and allow writing workouts, distance and active energy. A walk is saved when you end it.

No heart rate

Wear your Apple Watch, or connect AirPods that measure heart rate, and allow StepDesk to read heart rate in Health.

Try it without a pad

On the welcome screen choose Try demo, or turn on Settings, Demo mode. Demo walks are not saved to Apple Health.

Safety

Keep your pad's own remote or stop button within reach. StepDesk stops the belt over Bluetooth, which can drop.
